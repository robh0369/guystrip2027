# Boston — voice-over script

**Runs to:** `videos/boston.mp4` (0:46)
**Word count:** 105 · **Pace:** ~2.3 words/sec, conversational
**Tone:** Dry, unhurried, a friend telling you the plan. Not a travel ad. Let the
beats in the opening line breathe.

## Notes for the read

- **"seven-ten"** — say it as a time, not "seven ten p.m."
- **Fenway line** is the peak. Everything before it builds, everything after
  comes back down.
- **Last line** is the call to action. Land it flat and let the card hold.

## If the edit runs long or short

- **Cut first:** the Sunday line (0:40–0:43). The weekend still reads without it.
- **Add if short:** after the Fenway line — *"Oldest park in baseball, and small
  enough that there isn't a bad seat."*
- **Budget line, if the edit wants a number:** *"Call it a thousand a man, before
  flights."* (Full range on the page is $855–1,275.)
