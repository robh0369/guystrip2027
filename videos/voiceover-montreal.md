## Notes for the read

- **Montréal** — "mon-ray-AL". **St-Viateur** — "san vee-a-TUR".
  **Schwartz's** — "SHWARTZ-iz". **Saint-Paul** — English "saint", not "san".
- **"You will get soaked"** is the laugh line. Say it flat, like a warning, and
  leave a beat after it.
- **0:46–0:54** should sound like relief after the Saturday run-on.

## If the edit runs long or short

- **Cut first:** the second half of the Thursday line (from "dinner on Saint-Paul").
- **Add if short:** after the Crescent Street line — *"It gets genuinely crowded
  after eight, which is the point."*
- **Budget line, if the edit wants a number:** *"Call it a thousand a man, before
  flights."* (Full range on the page is $810–1,285.)
- **Do not** promise the island park or the fireworks — Parc Jean-Drapeau is
  inside the circuit fence that weekend and needs a race ticket.
