Flights

Both cities have nonstops into Logan, so nobody needs a connection. The trick is landing close enough together to share a ride and make dinner.

How to land together

Cincinnati picks first. CVG has a third as many flights as O'Hare, so the Cincinnati crew should find a Thursday flight that works and Chicago books around it. Going the other way round leaves somebody sitting at baggage claim for four hours.

Aim for a 4:00 to 7:00pm Thursday arrival. That puts everyone on the ground, into the city, and at a dinner table by 8:00 without burning a vacation day.

Same airline if possible. Delta and American both serve both cities. One airline means one terminal at Logan, which turns meeting up into a five-minute walk instead of a shuttle ride.

Sunday home: late afternoon or evening. Already agreed, and it leaves the 1:35 day game on the table for anyone flying out after 7:00pm.

Where We're Staying

No hotels · Registration rules

A house for the five of us, not hotel rooms. Boston is unusually strict about short-term rentals though, so the rule below is worth two minutes before anyone books.

The one rule that matters

Boston only allows short-term rentals in units where the owner actually lives. A registered "Home Share", meaning the owner's own place rented out while they're away, is legal and can sleep up to ten people. An investor who bought a condo purely to rent out is illegal, and the city takes those listings down. Sometimes days before check-in.

The check is simple: a legal listing shows a City of Boston registration number. If it isn't on the listing page, ask the host. No number, move on. Numbers can be verified against the city's registry, and the full rules are on Boston.gov.

Worth knowing: Cambridge and Somerville have the same owner-occupied rule, so hopping across the river doesn't get around it.

Which neighborhood

North End or downtown near the waterfront is the pick. It's a walk to dinner, a walk to the Freedom Trail, and a walk to the boats on Saturday, with Fenway a straight shot on the Green Line or a 15-minute Uber. Back Bay is the runner-up: closer to Fenway and the river, further from the harbor.

The Weekend

Four days

One main thing per day so nothing feels rushed. Every place below links out to a map and hours, and the Map button in the top bar shows how the whole thing fits together.

Day 1 · Thursday

20 May: Get in, eat well

Everyone lands at different times, so night one stays easy. Drop bags, walk around, get a big table.
Afternoon
Land at Logan. It sits about 15 minutes from downtown, which is a nice change from most airports. Blue Line or an Uber, both easy. Whoever gets in first grabs the dinner table.
6:00p
Drop bags and walk Hanover Street end to end. It's the Italian neighborhood and it's about four blocks long.
7:30p
Dinner in the North End. Bricco is the nicer one, Antico Forno does wood-fired everything and runs a little more casual. Both take reservations and both can seat five. Neptune Oyster is the one everyone recommends. It's excellent, but it's tiny and doesn't take reservations, so it's an hour-plus wait standing up. Not worth it with a group this size.
9:30p
Cannoli. Mike's and Modern sit across the street from each other and people have strong opinions. Get both and settle it.
10:15p
One more at Bell in Hand, open since 1795 and a five-minute walk. Touristy, but a fun first stop. For a proper cocktail instead, Ward 8 is right there too.
Day 2 · Friday

21 May: Freedom Trail, then Fenway

History in the morning, baseball at night. The whole day stays open because the game isn't until 7:10.
Game day
9:30a
The Freedom Trail, a red brick line set into the sidewalk that runs the whole route. Two and a half miles from Boston Common past Faneuil Hall and the Paul Revere House to the Old North Church. Flat, free, and easy to cut short at any point.
1:00p
Walk over the bridge to Charlestown for the USS Constitution and Bunker Hill, then a beer at the Warren Tavern (1780). If legs are shot by then, the ferry runs back across the harbor.
3:30p
Regroup at Trillium Fort Point. Best brewery taproom in the city, with plenty of room to stand around.
5:15p
Head to Fenway. Green Line to Kenmore. Pre-game at Bleacher Bar, built into the wall under the center field bleachers, with a garage door that looks straight out onto the field. It packs out about an hour before first pitch, so 5:30 at the latest. Otherwise Cask 'n Flagon across the street.
7:10p
Red Sox vs. Rangers at Fenway Park. Oldest park in baseball, and small enough that there isn't really a bad seat.
10:30p
Everyone spills onto Lansdowne Street after the game. Loretta's Last Call has live country and cheap beer, or Time Out Market is a food hall with a dozen vendors when nobody can agree on food.

Harbor day
10:00a
Late breakfast, nothing scheduled.
11:30a
Walk the Harborwalk from Long Wharf through Fort Point into the Seaport. Flat, right on the water, about two and a half miles.

The sail, for anyone who hasn't done one

Simpler than it sounds. Buy a ticket like a movie, show up at the dock 15 minutes early, and step onto a real tall ship with the sails up. A crew of three does the sailing. Passengers can help haul lines or just sit on deck with a beer and watch the skyline go by.

Two options, both good. Group vote before anyone books.

11:30a

1:35p

If Something Goes Sideways

Backups
If it's too windy to sail
Take the ferry to Georges Island from Long Wharf instead and walk around Fort Warren, a Civil War granite fort with dark tunnels that can be explored on foot with a flashlight. Ferries run Thursday through Sunday starting mid-May, so our dates work, but the 2027 season needs confirming once it's posted.
If it rains all Saturday
Drop the water day. Sam Adams brewery tour, the ICA out on the water, and a long lunch at Time Out Market.
If someone wants an actual hike
Blue Hills has real elevation and a view back at the skyline, but it needs a car, which is the one thing this plan avoids. Costs a half day plus a rental. Group decision.
If Friday's first pitch moves
Friday just slides with it. If it somehow ends up an afternoon game, swap Friday and Saturday completely: sail Friday, ballgame Saturday. Nothing else changes.
